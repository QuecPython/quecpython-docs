# Question Search 

This guide has two parts:

*   Tips for searching questions
*   Framework for question classification

##  **Tips for searching questions**

The search box in the upper right corner on the viewer is the fastest way to get answers to your questions.

The search box supports multiple keywords separated by spaces. Extract the keywords from the question and search, the necessary answer will be returned. 

For example, the question is: `What is the downlink bandwidth of CAT1? `

 It's recommended to search key words: `CAT1` and `downlink bandwidth`.
