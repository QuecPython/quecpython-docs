# FAQ on SMS

### **Does LTEonly support SMS function and rely on VOLTE? **

Depending on the specific model, most models will support the SMS function and do not necessarily need to rely on VOLTE.

How to verify whether it is supported:  The parameter `help('modules')` can be used  to check if SMS is available. Once it is available, which means it supports.
